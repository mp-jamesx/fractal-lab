# Editorial thumbnail instructions

Create one original 1200 × 675 live p5.js thumbnail for your assigned real Mirror Physics blog post. Use your entry in `briefs.json`, containing a shuffled palette, structure, and signal from Futuristic and lines. Read tags only; do not inspect moodboard images or other agents' sketches.

Site reference: https://www.mirrorphysics.com/blog . The parent inspected it: white editorial page, spacious layout, black sans-serif type, understated borders, a large featured post and clear article hierarchy. The artwork can use the dark palettes in the moodboard to sit boldly inside this restrained page. Do not copy existing thumbnails or use their images. The canonical article URL and a short verified conceptual brief are included in your entry. You may read that article and site for further context.

Art constraints: actual p5.js only; solid background then unfilled linework, curves, wire structures. No PNG/SVG/raster/image generation, photos, textures, text, gradients, or 2D chemical diagrams. Blog connection can be conceptual. Make a distinct, strong composition that reads at thumbnail scale. Avoid automatic six-panel grids or generic tiny molecule icons. Subtle motion is encouraged; no flashing.

CRITICAL chemistry: never invent flat chemical bond diagrams or randomly connect atoms. If any chemical structure is shown, import `drawMolecule` from `../chemistry.js`. It uses explicit nonplanar RDKit ETKDGv3/MMFF94 conformer coordinates and rigid 3D projection. Available names: caffeine, metronidazole, aspirin, cyclohexane. These are illustrative generated conformers, not measured structures or simulations. Use `drawMolecule(p,'caffeine',{x:600,y:337,scale:65,rx:.6,ry:time*.12,rz:.1,color:'#ddd',accent:'#6dd',hydrogens:true,weight:1.3,atomRadius:.14})`. Rotation must keep real xyz coordinates; do not flatten, stretch, or deform molecules. Molecules are optional. Purely abstract geometry can be procedural but must not be labeled as a real molecule/protein or MD output. Use caffeine for the latte article if depicting chemistry; metronidazole fits Rowan's article.

API: export `title`, `description`, `draw(p,seed)`, and optionally `animate(p,seed)` for frame rendering. Wrapper owns p5 instance, 1200×675 canvas, random/noise seeds, and 24fps animation. Draw once at setup; animate called on later frames, only when visible. Use p.millis()/1000 for time; redraw a full frame on animation. No imports except ../chemistry.js if needed. No setup/createCanvas in the module. Keep source self-contained. Write only the assigned sketch file.
